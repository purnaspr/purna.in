MY APPROACH

How I Work & Learn

01

I Learn by Building

I learn DevOps by actually building things, not just watching tutorials. I’ve worked on projects using AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins, Terraform, Ansible, Maven, and Tomcat. Along the way, I’ve faced failed builds, configuration issues, and deployment problems — and learned by fixing them.

02

Always Learning, Always Practicing

Most of what I know about DevOps has come from self-learning and hands-on practice. I started with Linux and Git, then moved into Docker, AWS, Jenkins, Terraform, Ansible, and Kubernetes. I’m still learning, but I make sure I practice what I learn by using it in projects.

03

I Prefer Automation Over Repetition

Whenever I find myself doing the same task again and again, I look for a way to automate it. I’ve used Terraform for infrastructure, Ansible for configuration, and Jenkins for CI/CD. As I continue learning, I want to take this further with GitOps and more automated cloud-native workflows.

04

I Don’t Stop at the Error

When something breaks, I try to understand why instead of simply starting over. During my projects, I’ve dealt with failed builds, Docker issues, server configuration problems, and deployment errors. Troubleshooting these problems has taught me to be more patient, systematic, and comfortable with debugging.
